A mid-sized firm that answers its own phone.

Eleanor Ashgrove and David Merritt started the firm in a two-room office on State Street with a simple rule: every client gets a named attorney, a written plan and a reply within one business day. That rule still holds.

Today 24 attorneys, 11 paralegals and 17 support staff work from our Boston headquarters and our Providence office. We are licensed in Massachusetts and Rhode Island and admitted to practice before the U.S. District Courts for the Districts of Massachusetts and Rhode Island and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the First Circuit.

Our attorneys and staff serve clients in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Tamil. In 2025 the firm contributed more than 1,200 pro bono hours to local legal aid organizations.

How working with us starts

From first contact to the first piece of work, most new clients are engaged within a week.

  1. Contact us

    Call, use the form below or email intake. We reply within one business day.

  2. Conflict check

    We confirm we don't represent anyone on the other side. This takes 24 to 48 hours.

  3. Consultation

    Meet your attorney in person, by phone, or on Zoom or Microsoft Teams.

  4. Engagement

    You receive a written engagement letter with scope, fees and who will work on your matter.

  5. Work begins

    You get access to the client portal and an update from your attorney at least every two weeks.

Good to know

  • Consultations are free for 30 minutes in Employment Law and Criminal Defense. Other practices charge $175 for a 60-minute consultation, which is credited to your first invoice if you hire us.
  • Employment discrimination and wage cases can be handled on contingency: 33⅓% of the recovery, or 40% if the case goes to trial. You pay nothing unless you recover.
  • Flat fee matters over $2,000 can be paid in up to six monthly installments with no interest.
  • We accept credit and debit cards, ACH bank transfer and checks.
  • A first-offense OUI flat fee covers representation through pretrial. Trial is quoted separately.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes, for Employment Law and Criminal Defense: the first 30 minutes are free. All other practices charge $175 for a 60-minute consultation, and that amount is credited to your first invoice if you hire us.

Do you handle personal injury or immigration cases?

No. We also do not handle bankruptcy, patent or tax controversy matters. We can suggest the Massachusetts Bar Association Lawyer Referral Service for these areas.

Can I meet my lawyer online?

Yes. Consultations and meetings are available in person, by phone, or by video on Zoom or Microsoft Teams.

Which states do you practice in?

Massachusetts and Rhode Island. We also practice in the federal courts for both districts and the First Circuit Court of Appeals. We do not take matters governed by other states' law.

How quickly will someone respond to me?

Within one business day for calls, emails and form submissions. If you have been arrested, call our 24/7 criminal defense line at (617) 555-0199.

Does contacting you make me a client?

No. Contacting us through this website, by email or by phone does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please don't send confidential details until we have completed a conflict check and you have signed an engagement letter.

Can the website assistant give me legal advice?

No. The assistant can answer questions about the firm, our services, fees and how to get started. Advice about your situation comes only from an attorney after you are engaged.

What languages do you speak?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Tamil. Tell us your preferred language when you contact us and we will match you with the right person.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, for flat fee matters over $2,000: up to six monthly payments, no interest.
